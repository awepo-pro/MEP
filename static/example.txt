The sun dipped below the rolling hills of the Cotswolds, casting a long, amber glow over the village of Lower Willow. Inside "The Rusty Anchor," the air smelled of damp wool, woodsmoke, and the yeasty promise of a well-poured ale. 

Arthur sat in his usual corner, his tweed jacket worn thin at the elbows. He wasn't waiting for anyone in particular, but in a place like this, one was never truly alone. The grandfather clock in the corner ticked with a rhythmic gravity, competing with the soft murmur of the rain that had just begun to lash against the windowpanes.

"Rough night out, Arthur?" Margaret asked, sliding a pint of bitter across the scarred wooden bar. 

"Standard Tuesday, Margaret," Arthur replied with a faint, wry smile. "The garden’s happy, even if my joints aren't."

The door creaked open, admitting a gust of cold air and a young woman clutching a saturated map. She looked entirely out of place in her bright yellow raincoat, a neon beacon against the pub’s sepia tones.
